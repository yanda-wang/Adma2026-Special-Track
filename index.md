
## Adam2026 Special Session Call for Papers

### Call for Papers

Recent advances in intelligent analytics, machine learning, and data-driven technologies are rapidly transforming healthcare and biomedicine. The increasing availability of large-scale healthcare data, including electronic health records, medical imaging, omics data, wearable sensor data, and population health data, has created unprecedented opportunities for developing intelligent methods and systems that improve clinical decision-making, personalized medicine, disease prevention, and healthcare management.

At the same time, healthcare and biomedical applications present unique challenges, such as data heterogeneity, temporal complexity, privacy concerns, interpretability requirements, and the need for reliable and trustworthy systems in real-world clinical environments. Addressing these challenges requires interdisciplinary research that combines advanced analytics, intelligent computing, data mining, and domain knowledge from healthcare and biomedicine.

This special session aims to provide a forum for researchers and practitioners from academia, industry, and healthcare organizations to present and discuss recent advances in intelligent analytics for healthcare and biomedicine. The session particularly encourages contributions on novel methodologies, real-world applications, intelligent healthcare systems, and translational studies that demonstrate practical impact in clinical and biomedical domains.


## Topics of Interest

The special session on Intelligent Analytics for Healthcare and Biomedicine invites original research contributions on intelligent methods, advanced analytics, and real-world applications for healthcare, biomedicine, and population health. Topics include, but are not limited to:

### Intelligent Methods and Analytics
- Machine learning and deep learning for healthcare
- Generative models and foundation models
- Large language models for clinical and biomedical tasks
- Data mining and knowledge discovery in healthcare
- Graph analytics and multimodal learning
- Explainable, trustworthy, and privacy-preserving analytics

### Healthcare Data Analytics
- Electronic health record (EHR) analytics
- Clinical outcome prediction and disease progression modeling
- Temporal and sequential health data analytics
- Population health and public health analytics
- Wearable, sensor, and mobile health analytics

### Biomedical Informatics and Computational Biomedicine
- Bioinformatics and computational biology
- Genomics, proteomics, and multi-omics analytics
- Drug discovery and drug repurposing
- Precision medicine and personalized healthcare

## Formatting Guidelines

Authors should prepare their manuscripts according to the formatting guidelines and submission instructions of the main conference track. Please follow the official LNAI (Lecture Notes in Artificial Intelligence) formatting requirements and submission policies provided on the main conference website.

#### Important Notes
- Papers must be written in English and submitted in PDF format.
- Submissions should be original, unpublished, and not under review elsewhere.
- All papers will undergo a peer-review process based on originality, technical quality, relevance, significance, and clarity.
- At least one author of each accepted paper must register and present the paper at the conference.
